About

Nicholas Stefan Georgescu is a mathematician, electrochemist, and engineer. He began college at fifteen, holds a B.S. in Biochemistry and a B.A. in Philosophy from Northern Kentucky University, and a Ph.D. in Mathematics from Case Western Reserve University, where his dissertation on the mathematical aspects of electrochemical transport phenomena produced more than ten peer-reviewed publications — more than double the typical output for the program.

After postdoctoral appointments at the University of Utah (Henry S. White lab) and McGill University (Janine Mauzeroll lab), he turned toward applied work: six years at Archer Daniels Midland, where he rose from Data Scientist to Senior Data Science Manager and presented forecasting and competitive-intelligence results directly to the CEO, replacing more than a million dollars per year of external vendor spend with in-house models; and then to Syrv.ai, where as Director of Data Science & AI he directs delivery across five concurrent enterprise engagements and authored the firm's reusable Claude Code delivery system.

Alongside this professional arc he has maintained a continuous private research practice in discrete mathematics — published on GitHub, PyPI, the AUR, and OEIS.
