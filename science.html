Science

Electrochemical transport phenomena. Peer-reviewed publications from work with the Scherson group at Case Western Reserve, the White lab at Utah, and the Mauzeroll lab at McGill.

Doctoral Thesis

Theoretical and Experimental Aspects of Electrocatalysis of the Oxygen Reduction Reaction and Related Systems. Case Western Reserve University, Department of Chemistry, January 2017. Advisor: Daniel A. Scherson.
Download PDF (4.7 MB)

Simulating Electrochemical Reactions in Python

Python-native adaptation of Honeychurch's Simulating Electrochemical Reactions in Mathematica: digital simulation of cyclic voltammetry via the finite-difference method with NumPy, SciPy, and Jupyter notebooks. Validated against Cottrell, Randles–Sevcik, and Levich.
